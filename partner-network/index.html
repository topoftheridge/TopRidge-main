A Client-First Partner Network Built Around Real Estate Relationships

Buying or selling a home creates dozens of moving parts — literally. Clients need movers, cleaners, junk removal, painters, locksmiths, and more, often within a tight timeline around closing.

The TopRidge Partner Network gives approved real estate professionals a private way to submit client service requests. TopRidge reviews each request and routes it to vetted local vendors who have been screened for quality, responsiveness, and professionalism.

This is not an open public lead form. The network is private and application-based to protect clients, agents, vendors, and the TopRidge brand.

Who The Partner Network Helps

Real Estate Professionals

Offer your clients a curated resource for trusted home services around closing — without personally vetting every vendor yourself. Submit a request through the private portal, and TopRidge handles the coordination with pre-screened local providers.

Clients (Buyers & Sellers)

Get connected with vetted, reliable home service providers recommended through your real estate professional. No cold-calling strangers, no guessing who to trust. Your agent submits a request on your behalf, and you receive qualified help during a stressful time.

Vendors & Service Providers

Get access to qualified, real-time client requests from approved real estate professionals — without paying for ads or chasing leads. Vetted vendors in the TopRidge network receive warm introductions from trusted agent partners who have already confirmed client permission.

Local Moving

Licensed and insured local movers for residential moves within the area, including loading, transport, and unloading.

Packing Help

How The Partner Network Works

A simple, private process designed to protect clients, agents, and vendors at every step.

01

Apply & Get Approved

Real estate professionals submit an application. TopRidge reviews each application and approves qualified partners based on licensing, brokerage affiliation, and professional standing.

02

Submit A Client Request

Approved partners use a private portal to submit service requests on behalf of their clients. Each request includes client permission confirmation, service details, and timeline information.

03

TopRidge Routes The Request

TopRidge reviews the request and matches it with a vetted local vendor from the network based on service type, location, availability, and quality standards.

04

Client Gets Connected

The client receives a warm introduction to the matched vendor. The vendor reaches out directly to coordinate scheduling, pricing, and project details.
